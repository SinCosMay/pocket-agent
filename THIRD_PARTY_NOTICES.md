# Source provenance

Native Android control contracts, adapters, mutation ledger and selected dispatch/schema logic are selectively reused from [ru-aish/pocket-mcp](https://github.com/ru-aish/pocket-mcp), commit `777add9fdf6193dd94ec9541d0278405dc0761f5`, at the repository owner's request. The upstream repository did not declare a license at extraction. Its transport, tunnel libraries, signing material and credentials are excluded. Reused source remains subject to its owner's rights; no new license grant over that material is inferred.

The Pi and Pi9 source/dependency pins and MIT notices are recorded under `runtime/` and `docs/RUNTIME.md`. Javet 6.0.2 is Apache-2.0; its Android AAR embeds Node and V8 with their upstream notices. Gradle wrapper files originate with Gradle (Apache-2.0). AndroidX test libraries are build/test-only (Apache-2.0).
